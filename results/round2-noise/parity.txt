`sab-<target>` is that target's own network namespace. Anything else is a host process sharing the news account -- production, usually -- and is listed because the budget is shared, not because it is the target.

A count above the configured budget is worth reading before it is worth acting on: a target can hold a connection outside its own pool accounting. A count well below it means the target never filled its allowance, which is a fact about the target rather than a broken parity rule.
